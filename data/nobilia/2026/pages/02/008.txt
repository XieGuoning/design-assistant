 Shelf insert Case CE* in 3 heights, suitable for kitchen, bathroom and living

 •       Shelf insert Case for side depth 461 / 561 mm
         in shelf unit heights 285 mm and 357 mm,
         usable depth: 401 mm, usable width: unit width - 88 mm, usable height: shelf insert height - 82 mm
         available for unit width: 300 - 1200 mm

 •       Shelf insert Case for side depth 350 mm
         in shelf unit heights 285 mm, 357 mm and 573 mm (with glass shelf),
         usable depth: 290 mm, usable width: unit width - 88 mm, usable height: shelf insert height - 82 mm
         available for unit width: 300 - 1200 mm,
         shelf unit height 573 mm is only available for unit width 300 - 600 mm

 •       Height, width and depth reductions are not permitted!

 •       By default, the open compartments of the unit elements are white on the inside.
         Therefore, the Case CE* shelf insert is required and must be included in the order.


 For Case designs, a Case unit element in the original front design is always combined with a Case shelf insert in the matching height.
 This allows the shelf insert to create a colour highlight within a conventional furniture design.
 You can also combine Case shelf inserts with our new dining table range.
 Table tops to match our Case inserts are available for all ET* / ETA* dining tables.
