     Notes on the decor process                Wood and stone décor reproductions are modelled after these natural materials. The décor directions can -
                                               particularly with large wood or stone textures - deviate from one worktop to another. Variations in colour and
                                               texture in the décor direction, also in the area of tongue and groove connections - are in no way grounds for
                                               complaint.
                                               Several décors exhibit prominant textures, the patterns of which only repeat in sizeable intervals and are thus,
                                               not visible on every worktop pattern or these illustrations.
                                               For this reason, you will find a depiction of the décor in its entire length at www.nobilia.de.


          Xtra material information            Our Xtra worktop décors impress with their particularly distinctive 3D surface texture, which creates an authentic
                                               looking natural stone or wood appearance appearance. Matching upright panels and niche cladding supplement
                                               the possibilities for surround designs in this look.
                                               Xtra worktops 38 mm thick are also characterised by an innovative multi-layered structure. The 1.2 mm thick
                                               laminate surface creates – on the one hand – the distinctive three-dimensional texture of the décor.
                                               Beneath the laminate lies a 9.4 mm thick innovative functional layer, which significantly increases the water
                                               resistance and impact resistance of the worktop.
                                               Glossy spots may develop on deep-matt décors from daily use due to sliding objects back and forth. This effect
                                               also occurs with décor 373 Grey slate reproduction and is comparable to the natural ageing that is typical of
                                               genuine slate. After some time, the décor takes on its own unique patina. This visual effect in no way limits
                                               usability and thus does not constitute a defect.

                                               The functional layer of the 38 mm thick Xtra worktop – which is impervious to swelling – enables flush-mounted
                                               installation of selected hobs and sinks in addition to conventional surface-mounted installation.


      Quartz material information              Quartz material worktops are distinguished by their high quality appearance and exceptional usability. Quartz
                                               material is a composite material that is made from natural quartz of varying grain size along with colour pigments
                                               and binding agents.
                                               Due to this process, quartz material retains a very natural appearance. This can naturally result in colour- and /
                                               or textural deviations within a single commission.

                                               Surface indentations, pigment accumulations and/or structural variations can occur with all composite materials.
                                               These are typical properties of this type of product and do not constitute grounds for complaint.
                                               Even without extra impregnation, quartz is waterproof. The surface is hygienic and without pores, which makes it
                                               exceptionally easy to maintain. In addition to conventional surface mounting, quartz worktops allow flush-mounted
                                               installation of selected hobs and sinks.


Xtra Ceramic material information              Xtra Ceramic worktops are characterised by their high-quality appearance and a scratch-resistant, pleasantly
                                               cool surface. The worktops are equally popular for kitchens and bathrooms thanks to their special aesthetic
                                               appearance and pleasant feel.

                                               The patented Xtra Ceramic worktops are moisture-resistant even without waterproofing. Their surfaces are
                                               hygienic and non-porous, making them particularly easy to clean.
                                               The worktop and niche thickness of 16 mm is achieved by attaching a substrate board made of recycled plastic
                                               (PET). This board is particularly light-weight and pressure-resistant.
                                               The worktops and niches are equipped with an impact-resistant design edge.

                                               In addition to conventional surface mounting, the Xtra Ceramic worktop allows flush-mounted installation of
                                               selected hobs and sinks.
