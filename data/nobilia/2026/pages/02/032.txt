Information for planning with bar handles
Designs with bar handles are particularly suitable for use in modern kitchen planning. The discreet shape of this handle design highlights the horizontal lines
of the kitchen.

Please take care during planning that the bar handle creates the appearance of a continuous band in the kitchen. This ensures a harmonious overall visual
impression.

Information
Not possible with diagonal end units and tall units with full-length door, as well as glass wall units Air, Square and Flat.

For certain appliance housings, an attached screw-type handle in handle position 18 is supplied instead (see information, chap. 6). When planning a hinged
unit, adjacent to a unit of greater depth (e.g. wall unit next to a tall unit), hinge the front away from the adjacent deeper unit, to avoid a handle clash.

Always plan elements with connection and door hinge on the wall side with a filler (minimum dimension = 20 mm). If a filler cannot be planned, change the
side of the door hinge.

It is not possible to position the bar handle on cooker housing units HUB-SL(-X) , or on all sink units with fixed panel.
