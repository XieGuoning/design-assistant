              Required information      Please state the handle number and the drill pattern in your order. This information is crucial. Not all handles and drill

                                        the individual order information per handle as well as the respective information given on the front pages.

    Handles subject to surcharge        The handles shown on the page M 2.22 can be ordered against a surcharge. The price will be invoiced per handle,
                                        independent of its length. The required handle lengths are automatically taken into account in the factory and adapted
                                        to the individual door sizes.
