                  Planning Instructions for Corner Solutions
                                  Base units | Corner solutions
                     Half corner cabinets (also XL height, maxi-height and highboards)

                     • Island designs of type UE(D)K(-X/-M) require a continuous base unit back panel (RKS*). The planning dimensions
                       must be specified with the order. Adjustments on site are not possible.
                     • Angle changes above or below 90° are generally possible, see surcharge UPE-VAR / 175.
                       Exceptions: Angle changes of 90 to 178° are possible for designs with a bar handle 240 / 430.
                     • Planning dimension changes from minus 20 mm to plus 100 mm are possible at the factory.
                     • A shortened carcase face for affixing to adjacent units is included in the scope of delivery.
                     • Door hinge side is also the face side.
UE...(-X /-M)        • Specify right or left, to ensure that the corner base unit is delivered with the correct hinge side.
                     • Planning a dishwasher adjacent to a corner unit requires an angle filler with a side depth of
                       561 mm (surcharge MP-GS).

                     Planning information for corner units with swivel pull-out

                     Note:
                     • The tablars swivel out ranging from 280 mm to 350 mm to the right / left and from 620 mm to 720 mm toward the
                       front upon opening, respective of unit size.
                     • Units with a swivel pull-out (UESA(-X/-M) / HESA(-X)) are not available adjacent to units of a greater depth
                       or stand-alone refrigerators with a depth of more than 585 mm!
                     • The hinged door must be opened at least 85° so that it is possible to swivel out the tablars.
                       Please check for handle depth & handle collisions if the side dimension of the filler is less than 70 mm.
