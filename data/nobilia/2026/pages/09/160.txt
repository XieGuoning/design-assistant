                Product features and planning information for sliding doors

                                                             • Aluminium frame in 014 Stainless steel appearance or 010 Black
                                                             • Decorative panels coated on both sides, mirror with safety film on rear side white, grey glass (TSG)
                                                             • incl. top track position holder, sealing brush and bottom track stopper in black or grey

                                                             • Track set, consisting of: Top and bottom tracks
