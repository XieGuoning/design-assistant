                     Planning and calculation information for sliding doors

            Measuring instructions    Detailed measurements are indispensable when planning sliding doors.
                                      Please check all walls, floors and ceilings for curvature.



               Measuring the width    Please measure the clear width of the room or passageway at three points (top - centre - bottom) and note all three
                                      values.
                                      Are all walls straight or do they curve outward or inward?
                                      The widest dimension is the width of your sliding doors system or the calculation basis for mounting in front of wall.
                                      The tracks are supplied precisely with the specified width dimension without a size allowance.




              Measuring the height    Please measure the clear height of the room or passageway at three points (left - centre - right) and note all three
                                      values.
                                      Does the ceiling sag or is it straight?
                                      The smallest dimension is the height of your sliding doors system for ceiling installation.
                                      The height can also be individually defined for mounting in front of wall. Please enter the total height of the sliding
                                      doors system here. During planning, please note that at least 100 mm clearance to the ceiling is required.




           Planning and calculation   When placing your order, please indicate the total width and height of the sliding door system
                       information    in addition to the set number.
                                      The tracks are supplied precisely with the specified width dimension without a size allowance.

                                      Please use the wall connection to achieve a clean finish, even with uneven walls.
                                      Add 25 mm in width for the wall connection.
                                      The adjustment range is ± 5 mm.

                                      For sliding doors without a stop toward a side wall, please use the cover caps for the top and bottom tracks.



            Information for designs   For sets with an open alcove please also indicate the width of the open alcove when placing your order.
                with an open alcove   This ensures that the corresponding door dimensions are automatically calculated correctly.

                                      As an alternative, you may also indicate the desired door widths when planning an open alcove - e.g. to hide planned
                                      built-in components behind the doors. Please specify the desired door widths when placing your order.
                                      This ensures that the width of the open alcove is automatically calculated.
