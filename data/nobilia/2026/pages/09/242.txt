                         Planning Instructions
                                  Planning information for
                              top shelves as cornice shelves /
                   accentuation of wall units using upright panel materials
           For safety reasons, the following information must be observed when planning kitchens with top shelves as cornice
           shelves and / or as accentuation of wall units using upright panel materials:

           • Planning of top shelves as cornice is permitted on all single door wall units as well as on all flap door, folding /
             lifting door, and swivel flap door units up to 1200 mm wide.

           • Planning of 50 mm cover panels on 2-door wall units is not permitted.

           • The total modification of individually suspended 2-door wall units is not permitted for structural reasons.

           • The total modification of single door wall units, as well as flap door, folding/lifting door, and swivel flap door
             units is only permitted in conjunction with 10 mm, 16 mm and 25 mm thick top shelves / upright materials.

           • The total modification of wall units with 50 mm thick materials is not permitted for structural reasons.

             Please note that a spacer tape (order no.: 9888) is required for the complete enclosure of wall units in order to
             prevent collisions between the front and horizontally mounted lower components.

             Please increase the height of side upright panels by 3 mm / 0.5 mm to accommodate the spacer tape.
