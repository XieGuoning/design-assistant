                                                               Coffee tables
Our versatile coffee tables combine stylish design and great function. With a total of four appealing models with two different widths, they are
sure to meet your individual interior design requirements. Depending on the model, our tables are available with different tabletop thickness and a
large variety of décors - low or at convenient height, designed to match our bevelled shelf units, mobile on wheels or with an elegant metal frame.
The simple yet modern design unifies storage space and style.
