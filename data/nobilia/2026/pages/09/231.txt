                 • Can be planned as LH or RH version.
                 • When planned at the end of run, the short side = 421 mm carcase depth becomes the visible end side.

                 • The delivery includes plinth.
                 • If this unit is used as a protruded solution, the protrusion must amount to 140 mm. Can be used, for instance,

UPE(-X/-M)      Base unit angle fillers
                • The planning dimensions of the unused corners are 650 x 650 mm.
                • The front filler (front angle filler) is 70 x 70 mm wide (ref. UPE-X/-M) or can be planned to have a length extension
                  of 50 - 170 mm (ref. UPE-V-X /-M).
                • Angle fillers are cut and bolted at the factory.
                  The space requirement must be specified with the order. Adjustments on site are not possible.
                • Planning dimension changes from minus 20 mm to plus 100 mm are possible at the factory.
                • Angle changes above or below 90° are generally possible, see surcharge UPE-VAR / 175.
                  Exceptions: Angle changes of 90 to 178° are possible for designs with a bar handle 240 / 430.
                • A shortened carcase face for affixing to adjacent units is included with the units.
                • When planning a dishwasher adjacent to the filler, consider the angle filler with a 561 mm deep side return
                  (surcharge-No. MP-GS).
                • The corner dimension 650 x 650 permits designs with drawers or front pull-outs next to the corner, no protruding
                  handles form an obstacle. Please note the respective handle height when creating your plan. It can be found
                  in chapter 2.
