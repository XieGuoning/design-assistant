                                     Planning information for wall panel




                       Wall panel   If you want to combine function and beauty in your living space, the customisable wall panel is the ideal solution.
              for custom planning   Its variable dimensions offer maximum freedom of design and allow it to be integrated perfectly into modern room
                 WPANW / WPANS      concepts.

                                    wall shelves, this panel is designed for perfect adaptation to its space.

                                    • Minimum dimensions: 380 mm x 200 mm
                                    • Available with horizontal grain WPANW (9416) or vertical grain WPANS (6418)

                                    • Wall panel 16 mm thick, incl. suspension fitting, 18 mm thick
                                    • Plastic profile, grey, as shadow strip
                                    • As an option, the wall panel is available at a surcharge with a notch at the bottom left or bottom right
                                      NVAUSK (4050)
                                    • To install plug-in wall shelves WB16* and WBF16*, include bag of fittings BB-WB (99212) in the order
                                    • Available in all carcase and worktop décors and NVM motifs with 3D texture

                                    • Invoiced per m², minimum price: 0.5 m²

                                    • If additional components are fitted, the manufacturer’s instructions must be observed
                                    • Load per wall panel: 50 kg
