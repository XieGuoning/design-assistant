                          Planning Instructions for Corner Solutions
                                         Base units | Corner solutions

UE(D) 80 / 90 (-X)           Diagonal corner unit with hinged door
UE(D)T 80 / 90 (-X /-M)      • Required floor space / Planning dimensions = 800 x 800 mm / 900 x 900 mm
                             • Door width: 300 mm / 450 mm
                             • Side depth: 561 mm
                             • The corner base unit can be taken apart on site.
                             • Changing and adding shelves at a later point is not possible.
                             Note:
                             • Do not plan ovens with protruding handles next to the corner base unit.
                             • Do not plan sink units and hobs adjacent to the corner base unit due to the cut-outs next to the tongue and groove joint.
                             • Do not plan lower fitted worktops adjacent to higher ones.
UE(D) 80 / 90 (-X)           • Not possible with the following handles using drill pattern 18: 370, 620, 650, 890.
