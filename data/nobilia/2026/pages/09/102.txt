                                  Genuine wood veneer stand-alone furniture
Our genuine wood veneer furniture in Native Oak brushed offers plenty of design options for a fully coordinated living space. With its warm presence and pleasant
feel, it makes any space more inviting and a true pleasure to live in. Whether as a base unit, sideboard, glass cabinet, wall unit, tall unit or midi unit - they are a
great addition to any room. Matching tables made of the same veneer add to the overall image and give the space a classy atmosphere. Genuine wood veneer
furniture is a great eye-catcher, not just as a complete arrangement, but also as stand-alone furniture that truly gets to shine when combined with the uni-coloured
unit elements of our latest collection.
