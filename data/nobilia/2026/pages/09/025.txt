                               Planning information for living space designs




Where do the living room start and where does the kitchen stop? The trend toward open-plan kitchens that transition into living or dining rooms continues
unabated. In addition, demand for holistic designs with unique personality and plenty of comfort is growing, particularly where small living spaces are concerned.
So it’s highly convenient if you can move seamlessly from designing the kitchen to designing the other living spaces without ever having to change the system
grid. However, our living room designs are also great solo performers and will look equally amazing in single room designs.
