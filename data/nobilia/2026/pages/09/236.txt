                  Corner solutions for 600 mm wide appliances
                  • Required floor space: 1050 x 1050 mm
                  • The wall unit area allows three different corner versions, see Figs. 1 to 3 with the attendant floor plans.
                  • In the base unit area, the corners are formed by the angle fillers type UPE135-2 or UPE135-2-V, which are screw-fitted
                    to the LH and RH cabinets.
                  • If a dishwasher is planned on the LH or RH side, the angle fillers are provided with a carcase side panel (MP-GS).




                  Niche rear panelling for 1050 x 1050 mm wide corner solutions
                  • Fig. 2 shows the corner canopy, consisting of the bottom section including the tiled cornice section (specify the laid
                    tiling dimension) and the top section which has to be adapted on site.
                  • Fig. 2 and 3 require niche back panelling. The floor plan sketches show the dimensions for the niche cladding and
           1258     corner wall units.
                  • The diagonal dimension of 1108 mm width accommodates two 135° corner open shelf units and one wall unit with
                    a width of 600 mm.
                  • Niche panelling, 1258 mm high, with 720 mm wall unit height (W-1)
                    Niche panelling, 1330 mm high, with 792 mm wall unit height (W-2)
                    Niche panelling, 1402 mm high, with 864 mm wall unit height (W-3)
                  • The side edges are rounded.
                  • In the event of niche panelling made on site, the corner dimension up to the front edge of the laid tiling is 791 mm
                    considering a 90° room angle.
                  • The niche panelling is suitable for tiling using a dispersion adhesive.
