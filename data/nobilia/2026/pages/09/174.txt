                                       Shoe cabinet and wardrobe elements
A place for everything! Wardrobe and shoe cabinets can be incorporated into our tall units to provide smart and practical storage solutions. An innovative and
ingenious idea for wardrobe and dressing room plans and for furnishings behind sliding doors.




                         Unit types      • HWGO* wardrobe unit: 1 wardrobe rail, available widths: 400 / 600 mm
                                         • HWG2O* wardrobe unit: 2 wardrobe rails, available widths: 400 / 600 mm
                                         • HWGAO* wardrobe unit: 1 wardrobe rail, tablar pull-outs, available widths: 400 / 600 mm
                                         • HWSO* shoe cabinet: Shelves in 2 depths (305 / 474 mm), available widths: 400 / 600 mm



               Extension elements        • Simply extend. Our new extension elements are designed to increase any wardrobe unit according to your
                                           requirements. The elements are available in all standard shelf unit colours (not available in colour 038 Reed green,
                                           212 Fjord blue, 254 Aqua) and in all tall unit heights. Each consists of two upright panels and a top shelf as well as
                                           matching accessories.
                                           The upright floor seal is available in the colours 010 Black and 014 Stainless steel appearance.
