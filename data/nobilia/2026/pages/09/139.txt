                  Planning information for pole-mounted shelving system
From the perfect kitchen to an inviting, holistic design. Whether it is used in the kitchen or as a wardrobe, for storage in the living room or bathroom or even as
a walk-in wardrobe – our pole-mounted shelving system is a striking conversation piece in any room. It offers you multifaceted and flexible planning options.
With innovative ideas, elegant design and countless practical details for integrated contemporary home interiors.




Pole-mounted shelving system           • Shelving system for versatile planning with aluminium support poles 25 mm wide
                                       • Available support pole colours: 073 Stainless steel finish, 010 Black
                                       • Available heights: 1512 mm, to fit highboard height
                                       				                 1584 mm, to fit XL highboard height
                                       				                 2016 mm, to fit tall unit height 1
                                       				                 2088 mm, to fit XL tall unit height 1 XL
                                       				                 1258 mm, to fit dresser unit height 1
                                       • Support pole depths: 150, 295 mm
                                       • Available plinth heights: 100, 150 mm
                                       • The aluminium support poles can be optionally equipped with LED light design MP-SRL (7930). One surcharge will
                                         be invoiced per support pole.
                                       • Cable routeing on the rear for up to 6 LED lines and cover profile are included in the scope of delivery.

                  Range of units       • A broad product range consisting of shelves, glass shelves, wardrobe rails, wooden boxes and planter boxes as well
                                         as wall-mounted base units. These are always planned between two support poles and are fastened with special
                                         connectors.
                                       • Pole-mounted shelves are available in thicknesses of 16 and 25 mm, are coated on both faces and have a polymer edge
                                         all around.
                                       • Available in all carcase colours as well as all worktop décors of décor group 1. Not available in Xtra décors and
                                         366 Black textured.
                                       • For a surcharge, the shelves can be equipped with LED built-in light Manila Plus (MP-RSBL) or an LED plant grow
                                         light (MP-RSBLP). LED-Startsets LSET-SMART36/60(-FB) are not included in the scope of supply, refer to accesso-
                                         ries list, lighting chapter.
                                       • All shelf dimensions are variable by 20 resp. 32 mm, in order to enable unit designs with 10 resp. 16 mm side upright
                                         panels. Designs with other upright panel thicknesses are not permitted.
                                       • As an alternative, 8 mm thick glass shelves are available.
                                       • All shelves and glass shelves come standard with connectors.
                                       • Pole-mounted base units are available in all ranges and carcase colours and can only be planned in conjunction with the
                                         pole-mounted shelving system. Pole-mounted base units must be equipped with a top shelf.
                                       • The base units are mounted to the wall with a heavy-duty suspension bracket. The connectors ensure support to the
                                         bottom of the hanging unit.
                                       • Available carcase heights: 360 mm, 432 mm
                                       • In addition, wall units of every height can be planned between two shelving system support poles. For secure
                                         connection to the support poles, unit connectors RSSK are automatically confirmed and supplied with the order - and
                                         invoiced.
