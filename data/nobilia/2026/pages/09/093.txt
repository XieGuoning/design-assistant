                           Wall and tall units with framed glass door Ambia
Experience a whole new dimension of modern interior design:
Our Ambia glass units combine proven functionality with an exciting approach to design. Key features are asymmetrical glass cut-outs, which break up the classic
unit image and set stylish accents.
Additional design elements such as an accent rear panel in the unit or wall panels which can be freely attached to the wall behind the furniture create a cosy
atmosphere with contemporary style.
Optional atmospheric lighting also ensures even more ambiance and elegantly stages the glass cut-outs and content.
This creates an eye-catcher, which impresses during the day as well as at night.
