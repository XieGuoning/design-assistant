                                     Planning information for media furniture
Our media furniture is not only a visual highlight, but also provides a simple way to store media accessories. The flexible planning options combined with individual
and practical details are the perfect addition for an integrated living room design.




          Media console and panel         The media console is ideal for hi-fi units or a soundbar. Cables are guided through an offset back panel and a cable feed-
                                          through. The media panel can be combined with a base unit or the media console. This solution can be used to mount
                                          a TV on the wall and neatly hide any inconvenient cables.
                                          Thanks to the media panel’s reinforced structure, it is even possible to mount TVs with a pivot arm. The panel can be
                                          optionally equipped with MP-MEDIPL ambiance lighting. This doesn’t just make the furniture look good, but also adds a
                                          warm atmosphere to the entire room.
