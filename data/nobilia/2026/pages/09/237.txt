                                                Planning Instructions
                                                  Planning information for
                                                  independent hot plates
KKB / DBKI / NDBKI               For operation of conventional or induction hot plates in 38 mm worktops directly above a drawer, pull-out or hinged
                                 door, a heat protection panel (KKB, DBKI, NDBKI) must be installed for safety reasons, in so far as the appliance does
                                 not have its own protection panel.

                                 Please take the following points into consideration when planning your kitchen and include the respective information
                                 with your future orders.


                                 As a rule when ordering:
                                 • For reasons of safety and product liability we are obligated to confirm and deliver the respective heat protection
                                   panel (KKB, DBKI, NDBKI) with all orders that include the hot plate.
                                 • For this reason, it is not possible to delete the heat protection panel!
                                 • With                 kitchens, the recessed handle is provided with an additional milled opening for ventilation
                                   (MP-NAUSI). Milling is not necessary for appliances from the BSH and Electrolux group or for hot plates of the
                                   brands Miele, Bora and Elica.
                                 • If the hot plate is not an integral component of the order, please inform us in writing, whether the required heat
                                   protection is also to be supplied by the third-party supplier / appliance manufacturer.
                                 • If this information is missing from your order, we are obligated to confirm and deliver the respective heat protection
                                   panel.


                                 Observe during kitchen planning:
                                 • For planning hot plates across several base units, adjacent units with a width of 150 and 200 mm can be planned
                                   without a heat protection panel, as long as the central hot plate unit has a minimum width of 500 mm and is
                                   equipped with the respective heat protection panel.
                                 • Planning induction hot plates above 2-door base units or half corner units is not permitted, since the protection
                                   shelves (DBKI) cannot be adapted at the factory nor on site. Plans for ceramic glass hot plates with thermal radiation
                                   above half corner units will be reviewed individually based upon the respective plan.
                                 • Planning induction hot plates above 2-door base units or half corner units is not permitted, since the protection
                                   shelves (DBKI) cannot be adapted at the factory nor on site.
                                 • For safety reasons it is not permissible to install hot plates above built-under appliances (dishwashers, fridges, etc.).
