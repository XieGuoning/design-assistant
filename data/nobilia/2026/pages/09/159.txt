                 Product features and planning information for sliding doors




One of the major advantages of sliding doors is their space-saving design. Many houses and apartments have alcoves and corners which simply can’t
accommodate conventional furniture and are therefore left bare. Sliding doors are extremely versatile for making effective use of limited space. But they are also an ideal
solution for anyone who wants to waste as little space as possible in larger rooms or wants to create a spacious impression. Moreover, sliding doors are great as room
dividers, for built-in closets or walk-in closets. Sliding doors are perfect for systematically presenting or hiding furnishings, thereby creating multifunctional room designs.
Thanks to the new option of mounted in front of wall, the sliding doors can also be used as room doors, for example, and therefore offer the possibility of a
continuous room design.
