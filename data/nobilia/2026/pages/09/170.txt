Planning and calculation notes for sliding doors for mounting in front of wall
We recommend 2 sliding door brakes per door for sliding doors wider than 750 mm.
Max. 1 sliding door brake can be installed for doors with a width below 750 mm.
In this case, a tilt protection must be installed.
Please specify the number of sliding door brakes per door.
If the order does not include any information on the closing direction, the system will be installed according to the information for the sliding door sets in the
catalogue.
