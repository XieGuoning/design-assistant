           Cable routing duct**     Planning the cable routing duct is a very important part of the kitchen order.
                                    Dishwashers, plinth drawers, or refrigerators with plinth refrigeration units or similar cannot be planned atop the flooring
                                    in the cable routing duct area. Also, the cable routing duct cannot be planned beneath units with a plinth height of 70 mm.
                                    Exception – cable routing duct in floor screed, on the basement ceiling or directly behind the appliance and vented
                                    to the outside.
                                    Then the plinth height is not relevant.
