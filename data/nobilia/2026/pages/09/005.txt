                                  Planning information for bathroom designs




The trend toward individually designed interiors with a homely character continues into the bathroom. With this, so does the demand for perfect storage space,
organisation and ergonomic furnishings. Simply put, the need for convenience has substantially increased.
The quality features of our furniture make it ideally equipped to meet these requirements. TÜV Rheinland successfully tested our bathroom base units
according to the legal requirements of the RAL-GZ 430 quality standards for kitchen and bathroom furniture.

                 The product range        • Base units for bathroom designs are available in four carcase heights: 288 / 360 / 432 / 576 mm.
                                          • Sideboards and Midi units are available as occasional furniture in the heights 864, 1152, 1296 and 1584 mm.

                                          • For handleless bathroom designs, LINE N base units are available in 4 carcase heights: 360 / 432 / 554 / 603 / 630 mm.
                                          • LINE N sideboards and Midi units are available in the heights 720, 1152, 1247, 1296 and 1584 mm.

                                          • Base units and occasional furniture for bathroom designs are available in all ranges and all carcase colours.
                                          • Base units are 461 mm deep as a standard. The side depth of the occasional furniture is 350 mm.
                                          • Depth reductions on bathroom base units to side depth 350 mm (side depth 371 mm for bathroom open shelf base
                                            units) are available without a surcharge for the units identified accordingly.
                                          • A side depth of 561 mm (582 mm side depth for shelf base units) is available for units identified accordingly at a
                                            surcharge (MP-BUST56) incl. support console.

                                          • The codes are as follows:
                                            Bathroom base units 		                     BU* -29 / -36 / -43 / -58
                                            Bathroom vanity units		                    BW* -29 / -36 / -43 / -58
                                            Bathroom sideboards		                      BS* -86
                                            Bathroom Midi units		                      BM* -115 / -130 / -158

                                          • For the handleless LINE N range, an N is added before these abbreviations.
                                            Bathroom base units 		                  NBU* -36 / -43 / -55 / -63
                                            Bathroom vanity units		                 NBW* -36 / -43 / -55 / -60 / -63
                                            Bathroom sideboards		                   NBS* -72
                                            Bathroom Midi units		                   NBM* -115 / -125 / -130 / -158

                                          • The units can be covered using worktops or top shelves as well as commercially available hand basins
                                            and countertops.

                                          • Special vanity units are available in every height for inset and countertop mounting of hand basins. These are 		
                                          equipped with a metal crossbar. Pull-outs and drawers on these units are equipped with a wooden back panel for
                                            on-site cutting of an odour trap recess. For a surcharge SIA1WT / SIA2WT these odour trap recesses can also be 		
                                          cut at the factory.

                                          • For extra protection against moisture, bathroom units can be equipped with a waterproof bottom
                                            shelf for a surcharge MP-WP.

                                          • For selected accessories, washbasins and taps, refer to the accessories
                                            section of this sales manual.

            Installation information      • The units are equipped with a heavy-duty fitting for wall mounting.
                                          • The heavy-duty mounting fitting ensures secure mounting in honeycomb brick,
                                            sand-lime brick and concrete.
