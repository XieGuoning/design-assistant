                                              Chairs, bar stools and tall stools
Our own range of chairs is the perfect addition to your kitchen or dining room design. With four distinctive frame designs and six
top-selling colours, our chairs will look great in any room. The Swing, Turn and Cross models all come with a chair and a matching armchair with armrests. A
version of Turn II is also available with a modern cut-out in the backrest. The Cross series also contains an additional bar stool with a seat height of 650 mm
and a tall stool with a seat height of 790 mm. This lets you furnish your dining room and kitchen counter in one consistent style. Thanks to its stackable design,
our Move chair is both flexible and space-saving.
