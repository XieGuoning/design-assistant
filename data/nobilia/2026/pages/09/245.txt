Explanation of Symbols
       Hot water 1/2“ angle valve

       Cold water 1/2“ angle valve with 3/4“ threaded dishwasher connection

       DN 50 drain

       Earthed plug, 230 Volt

       Cooker connection N / 3 x 220 Volt (3 phase current)

       Exhaust air duct depending upon appliance (Ø 125 mm / 150 mm)

       Gas connection (gas socket) do not plan behind the gas cooker

       Do not plan any installation behind a dishwasher or washing machine
       Water connections must be planned so that they are accessible from the front!
       The drain height for washing machines is measured from the bottom edge of the appliance and must be raised accordingly.
       For the height, refer to the appliance operating manual!

       For Beko-dishwasher: Drain hose must be positioned at a height of 500 mm away from the lowest edge of the appliance.



Dimensions: top edge of finished flooring in mm, all height dimensions are
based on a 100 mm plinth height.

Note the connection and installation instructions of the applicance manufac-
turers for integrated electrical appliances.
