                             Planning Instructions
                                  Tips for protruding designs with UFK units
              1. The standard protrusion for UFK units is from 0 to 5 mm. UFK types with a width of 1000 to 1200 mm require a full-depth
                 top shelf for stabilisation. ADB10* is only permitted up to a max. UFK width of 1000 mm.

              2. If the wall gap for dresser units is 15 to 20 mm, the spacer plate bag 99190 is included and invoiced as a standard
                  for each unit.

              3. Furthermore, UFK units can be planned to protrude up to 50 mm.
                  In this case, please observe the following information:
                  a. The UFK unit must be planned between two posts that reach the floor. This could be tall units or end uprights that
                     reach the floor and wall (min. 16 mm thick).
                  b. A maximum of one UFK unit can be planned to protrude between two posts.
                  c. A bracket set WINS (99139) is required for fastening. This comes standard as part of the delivery and is invoiced.
                  d. For stabilisation of UFK units from 1000 to 1200 mm wide, an additional top shelf is required – which covers the
              		 total depth (unit depth + protrusion dimension).

              4. If UFK units are stabilised with a top shelf, these can be planned to protrude up to a maximum of 150 mm. In this
                 case, the same planning information applies as described under point 3. a - c.
                 On UFK types up to and including 600 mm width, no top shelf is required.

              5. By default an ADB planned underneath UFK types is a trigger for the spacer band 9888, which is invoiced.
