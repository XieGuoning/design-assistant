                           Planning Instructions for Corner Solutions
                                           Wall units | Corner solutions
                              Planning possibilities for corner wall units
                              • All corner base units can be variably matched with corner wall units in both wall unit heights (576 / 720 / 792 / 864 mm).
                              • The vertical line should always be continued in line with the base units or at least coincide again with the next unit.
                              Take note if protruding handles are planned:
                              • When the diagonal corner wall unit has a RH hinge, the wall unit must also be hinged on the right-hand side.
                              • When the diagonal corner wall unit has a LH hinge, the wall unit must also be hinged on the left-hand side.
                              Take note when placing cooker hoods or tall units next to the corner wall unit 650 x 650 / 800 x 800 / 900 x 900 mm:
                              • The first door that needs to be opened must not be directly next to the cooker hood or a tall unit, as it would not be
                                possible to open this door due to the depth of the cooker hood or tall unit!

                              • The 900 x 900 mm planning dimension can be obtained for instance by a 650 x 650 mm diagonal corner wall unit
