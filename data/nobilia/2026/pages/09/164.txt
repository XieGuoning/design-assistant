                                                                Sliding door sets

                       Order per set      • Each design and order is based on one of the following 12 sliding door sets.
                                          • These cover all conventional design solutions in three variants:
                                            - as a fully enclosed system
                                            - as design with an open alcove
                                            - to be mounted in front of wall
                                          • The set serves as a planning aid for your order.
                                          • The price for your order is calculated from the individual items in the set parts list.
                                            The set itself does not have a price of its own.


                   Set components         • As a rule, all sets consist of 2 components:
                                            1. Track set, consisting of bottom and top tracks and top track supports if required
                                          		 invoiced per started 100 mm and the number of top track supports if required
                                          		 Please indicate the profile colour in your order. 014 Stainless steel appearance or 010 Black
                                            2. Sliding door(s) incl. top track position holder, sealing brush and bottom track stopper
                                          		 The price of a sliding door is calculated from a fixed base price per sliding door GGT (1404) plus a price per m2
                                          		 GT120 (1401).


                 Panel-dividing rail      • A surcharge per door is invoiced for sliding doors with a panel-dividing rail.
                                            In this case, the doors are visually divided into thirds in a symmetrical manner.
                                          • If continuous and divided doors are combined in a set, please indicate which door is to be divided when placing
                                            your order.
                                          • A surcharge per door is invoiced for a different décor in the centre panel.
                                          • Please specify the different décor for each door.


    Situation-specific accessories        • Accessories which depend on the specific installation situation must be ordered in addition:
                                            - Sliding door brakes, invoiced per item.
                                            - Tilt protection (is automatically invoiced for sliding doors under 750 mm with a sliding door brake)
                                            - Wall connection, invoiced per started 100 mm.
                                            - Cover caps for top and bottom tracks incl. top track stopper
                                            - Top track end and top track centre supports
