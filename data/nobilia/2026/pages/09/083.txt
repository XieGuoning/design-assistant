                           Planning information for bevelled upright panels
With our bevelled upright panels combined with top shelves of 10 mm thickness, you can create intricate yet airy bathroom and living room designs with the minor
front overhangs. The upright panels are 16 mm thick and are available in all carcase colours and all front colours in the Easytouch 945 range.
