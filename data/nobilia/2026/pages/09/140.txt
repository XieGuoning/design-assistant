                   Design ideas for pole-mounted shelving system

                                               Our pole-mounted shelving system offers versatile planning
                                               options for a wide variety of room situations. Several planning
                                               examples can be found here and on the following pages.
                                               The sample designs do not contain pole-mounted lighting or any
                                               matching accessories. Support pole lighting can be found on the
                                               following pages.
                                               The LED starter or follow-up sets can be found in our accessories
                                               list in the lighting chapter.

                                               To connect the aluminium support poles to the wall at the
                                               bottom, it is absolutely necessary to plan a base unit or shelf
                                               at a maximum distance of 600 mm from the bottom edge of the
                                               floor!
