                                            Unguided circulating air or duct-guided circulating air
                                            Most appliances can often be installed in different ways. Duct-guided or unguided circulating air triggers completely
                                            different unit designs.




                                            Unguided circulating air
                                            With unguided circulating air, the hot plate unit is supplied with a continuous back panel; the vapours are guided down-
                                            wards behind the unit. Important: A sufficiently large return flow opening must be ensured on site by cutting
                                            down the plinth, plinth ventilation grille or louvre plinth.
Downdraft extractor fan,
circulating air, unguided
(closed back panel)


                                            Duct-guided circulating air
                                            With duct-guided circulating air, the hot plate unit is supplied with a half-height, reversible back panel.
                                            This enables easy installation and connection of the ducts. Please indicate the planned air duct when placing your
                                            order. Important: No unit can be generated without this information!
