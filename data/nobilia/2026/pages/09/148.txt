                                                Sign Emotion railing system




Sign Emotion is a modular railing system with a wide variety of different accessories for kitchens, bathrooms and living rooms. Shelves of different widths and
heights and the extensive range of accessories turn Sign Emotion into a flexible universal talent that unites storage space and design. All elements can be
attached and detached at any point and can be moved along the rail.
