            • If the APN / APD or Slim Line APL are planned with an overhang to the rear of >300 mm, a side or centre support
              is required!
            • The support can be provided by floor-depth upright panels (16, 25, 50 mm) which must be planned along the entire
              worktop depth. As an alternative (except for Slim Line) support legs (1 centre support leg or 2 support legs on the
              outside) can be included in the plan (see fig. 2).
            • The max. overhangs for quartz and Xtra Ceramic cannot be increased by supporting elements.
