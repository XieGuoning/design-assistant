                Product features and planning information for sliding doors




               Elevated sliding doors When planning raised sliding doors, the anti-lift device AUSHS 1487 is required and is automatically also confirmed,
                                      invoiced and supplied.
                                      A stop toward the wall and / or adjacent units is required on the left-hand and right-hand side!




    Sliding doors for mounting in        To position sliding doors in front of the wall, the top track must be secured to the wall with top track end GSWSTE 1408
                      front of wall      and top track centre supports GSWSTM 1409.
                                         Mounted in front of wall is only permitted with the single-track profile.




              Visually divided fronts    At a surcharge GTS 120 (1406), the doors are also available in a divided design with a panel-dividing rail.
                                         The centre panel can also be delivered in a different décor.
                                         If continuous and divided doors are combined in a set, please indicate which door is to be divided
                                         when placing your order.
