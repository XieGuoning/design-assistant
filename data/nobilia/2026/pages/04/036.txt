Special considerations for designing quartz worktops with a thickness of 38 mm
              General information       • Rounding is not possible.
                                        • Maximum worktop overhang 250 mm.
                                        • Visible edges will be invoiced a surcharge for additional edge processing APQ-K38.
                                        • Cut-outs for sinks and hot plates cannot be made on site.

Information on subsequent processing of quartz
              General information      • Subsequent processing of quartz on site is only possible with suitable tools.
                                       • Minimum requirements:
                                         • Drill: Diamond drill bit for tiles, granite, stoneware, ceramics
                                         • Saw: Flex hand-held angle grinder; saw blades: Diamond cutting wheels (dry) for tiles, granite,
                                           stoneware, ceramics
                                         • Polishing machine: Hand-held sanding pads/polishing pads, galvanised diamond for stone, glass, ceramics
                                         • Personal protective equipment is required for subsequent processing of quartz on site.
                                       • Minimum requirements:
                                         • Dust mask, protection rating FFP2




Special considerations for designing quartz niches and upstands
              General information      • All connections to the wall and adjacent units must be sealed. For this purpose,
                                         the quartz mounting set APQ-MSET is automatically confirmed, supplied and invoiced for each consignment.
                                       • A 1 mm gap from adjacent structures and for butt joints must be included in the calculation.
                                       • A +2 mm overhang must be included for each visible side.
