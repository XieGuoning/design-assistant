Planning information for Xtra Ceramic worktops
             General information      • Tables, breakfast bars and worktop extensions are not possible.
                                      • Fastening support consoles is not possible.
                                      • Maximum worktop overhang 300 mm.
                                      • No connection with structure for window reveal!
                                      • Worktop joints must be on the carcase side or are supported by supplied mounting plates.
                                      • 1 mm air gap between worktop joints is automatically generated.
                                      • All connections to the wall and adjacent units must be sealed. For this purpose, the Xtra Ceramic mounting set
                                        APC-MSET is automatically confirmed, supplied and invoiced for each consignment.
                                      • 1 mm inward offset for each side for double-sided connections to wall and / or adjacent units is generated automatically.
                                      • 2 mm worktop overhang per unconnected visible side is generated automatically.
                                      • Corner connections with angular deviations cannot be adapted on site using an extra-deep worktop. Angle modifications
                                        possible for surcharge UPE-VAR, APC-PST and APC-A with min. 67.5°.
                                      • Visible sides will be invoiced a surcharge for additional edging APC-K16.
                                      • Notches are not possible with additional edging APC-K16.
                                      • Cut-outs for sockets cannot be made on site.
                                      • Rounding is not permitted.
                                      • For complicated Xtra Ceramic designs, a subsequent order after the completed kitchen installation is possible. In this
                                        exceptional case it is permitted - if indicated in the first OC - to perform an additional block pricing.

          Information for designs     • Hobs with a cut-out dimension of 560 mm width are permissible for the cooker housing units HUB-SL(-X),
                   with hot plates      HUS-SL60-M, HUS90(-X), NHU60(-X) and NHUM60(-X ) and for all hot plate units 600 mm width or more.
                                      • Cooker housing units and hot plate units of 600 mm width require the Xtra Ceramic installation set APC-SET60.
                                      • Hot plate units and cooker housing units of 800 mm width or more require the Xtra Ceramic installation set APC-SET80.
                                        Hobs with the cut-out dimensions 750 - 780 mm in width are only permissible for the cooker housing unit HUS90(-X)
                                        and for hot plate units of 900 mm width or more.
                                      • Designs of built-in cookers or ovens under hot plates are only possible using cooker housing types HUB-SL(-X) /
                                        HUS-SL60-M and HUS90(-X).
                                      • Drawers under hot plates are only possible for designs involving hot plate units KS*(-X), K2SA*, KS2A*(-X), SKU* in
                                        combination with the low frame MP-NIEZA.
                                      • During installation, please adhere to the installation information and installation depths provided by the corresponding
                                        manufacturers.
                                      • Cut-outs for hot plates cannot be made on site.
