Processing on Xtra Ceramic worktops and niche claddings
              General information       • Binding sketches with specific dimensions are required for order clearance of plans with Xtra Ceramic worktops.
                                          Subsequent processing on site is only possible with suitable tools for ceramic material.


Information on subsequent processing of Xtra Ceramic
              General information       • Subsequent processing of Xtra Ceramic on site is only possible with suitable tools.
                                        • Minimum requirements:
                                          • Drill: Diamond drill bit for tiles, granite, stoneware, ceramics
                                          • Saw: Flex hand-held angle grinder; Saw blades: Diamond cutting wheels (dry) for tiles, granite, stoneware,
                                            ceramics
                                          • Polishing machine: Hand-held sanding pads/polishing pads, galvanised diamond for stone, glass, ceramics
                                          • Personal protective equipment is required for subsequent processing of quartz on site.
                                        • Minimum requirements:
                                          • Dust mask, protection rating FFP2


Special considerations for designing Xtra Ceramic niches and upstands
              General information       • All connections to the wall and adjacent units must be sealed. For this purpose, the Xtra Ceramic mounting set
                                          APC-MSET is automatically confirmed, supplied and invoiced for each consignment.
                                        • A 1 mm gap from adjacent structures and for butt joints must be included in the calculation.
                                        • A +2 mm overhang must be included for each visible side.
