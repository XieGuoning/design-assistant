           Planning information for glass and mirror niche cladding NVG120
                General Information       • Maximum dimensions: height from 250 to 1200, width from 250 to 2500 mm.
                                          • Minimum dimensions for NVG with processing: 250 x 250 mm.
                                          • Minimum dimensions for NVG without processing
                                               45 to 90 mm height x 200 to 1500 mm width
                                               91 to 150 mm height x 150 to 2000 mm width
                                               151 to 1250 mm height x 150 to 2500 mm width
                                          • Binding sketches with specific dimensions are required for order clearance.
                                          • A written confirmation of the manufacturer‘s sketch is required from the customer for production clearance.
                                          • Subsequent processing on site is not possible.
                                          • With elaborately planned glass niche cladding it has proven successful on the market - for reasons of fitting accuracy - to
                                            allow a subsequent order after the completed kitchen installation. In this exceptional case it is permitted - with provision of
                                            the first OC - to invoice an additional kitchen block.

           Notching or Chamfering         • The minimum distance between notches and the outer edge is 120 mm.
                           NVG-A          • The notch height must not exceed half of the glass height and must be a maximum of 200 mm deep.
                                          • The standard inner radius is 10 mm, the edge is unpolished.
                                          • Larger radii up to 600 mm are possible and must be specified with the order.
                                          • The edge will be supplied polished for an inner radius upwards of 60 mm.
                                          • For sharply angled corners, the minimum angle must not be smaller than 30°.




                           Rounding       • There is no minimum distance to the outer edge.
                             NVG-R        • The standard edge version is polished.
                                          • Please specify the radius in your order.

     Drill holes for electrical sockets   • The diameter of the drill hole is 68 mm.
                              NVG-SD*     • The minimum distance to the outer edge, to other finishing details or the
                                            next free drill hole is 35 mm.
                                          • The only exceptions are with double, triple, quadruple or quintuple drill
                                            holes for electrical sockets. The space from centre to centre is 71 mm. The
                                            centre partition is drilled.




                           Drill holes    • Drill holes with a diameter of less than 21 mm are only available in the
                            NVG-BO*         fixed dimensions 5 / 6 / 7 / 8 / 10 / 12 and 21 mm.
                                          • The minimum distance to the outer edge is 35 mm.
                                          • Drill holes must be measured from the hole edge and spaced apart a
                                            minimum of 1x the diameter of the largest hole.




                  Slotted drill holes     • Maximum dimensions: width from 22 to 600 mm. Height from 22 to 100 mm
                         NVG-BOL*         • The minimum distance to the outer edge is 35 mm.
