                     Planning information for base units in special heights
               General Information    • Units in special heights are available in three carcase heights: 288 mm / 360 mm / 432 mm
                                      • The types can be used multifunctionally and stacked on top of each other, for example.

                                      • Base units in special heights can be planned with all plinth heights.
                                      • For the plinth heights 70, 100, 150, 200 mm, the base units are supplied with plinth panels and returns.
                                      • The 20 mm plinth height is supplied without a plinth panel.
                                      • The base unit floor is drilled at the factory for the 20 mm plinth height, the adapters are installed on site.
                                      • If base units are planned stacked on top of each other at a special height, the 20 mm plinth height must also be
                                        ordered.
                                      • The designs are particularly suited to residential solutions, e.g. as a bench seat or lowboard for TV equipment.
                                      • An upright panel is necessary for planning adjacent to a dishwasher.
