Dear Business Partner!

Please find our sales documents enclosed for the 2025/2026 collection.

We’ve made our 2026 all about innovative product designs and a wide range of new design options. To reflect this choice, this year’s motto
is “Design meets Concepts”. Our new features are characterised by natural colours and textures, integrated designs, well-thought-out
solution concepts and fascinating movements.

With 13 new fronts, we are expanding our collection to include the latest trend colours and geometries. In soft natural tones such as Sand,
Macchiato, Taupe grey, Coffee, Stone blue and Olive, with textured surfaces or as framed fronts – our new fronts set standards in terms
of design, feel and quality.
Look provides new impetus in price group 1. The fine texture with a ceramic look lends the design a natural radiance and lively excitement.
Look is available in the popular Sand textured colour and the new Coffee textured colour. Coffee presents warm, elegant naturalness.

Since its market launch last year, Softline has been a hit with its design in Lacquer, perfect honed and will also be supplemented by the
colours Sand and Coffee for 2026. Thanks to its high-quality feel, robust coating and innovative anti-fingerprint properties, the programme
impresses in everyday use – a strong plus point in sales talks. Real lacquered fronts with a high-gloss finish also remain in vogue: We
are therefore expanding Novalux to include the colour Taupe grey. The warm grey tone with a light brown component creates a high-
quality and modern ambience.

Macchiato is a new colour that lies somewhere between Sand and Taupe grey. The modern brown/grey version complements the successful
Laser program in price group 1 and Senso in price group 3. As a new design colour, the shade is available as a carcase colour and worktop
décor and also extends the frame fronts segment.

With six new frame fronts and coordinated design elements, our cottage style segment is versatile and modern, while at the same time
opening up additional potential with a new, attractive entry-level price. The five-part Frame front combines modern cottage style design
with an elegant, fine wood texture and offers an attractive entry into price group 4 with four on trend colours. The result is a fresh cottage
style look – modern, elegant and surprisingly versatile. The four colours White, Macchiato, Stone blue and Olive create a portfolio that
appeals to a wide range of tastes and offers a variety of design options. Maximum suitability for everyday use and easy to clean thanks to
the anti-fingerprint coating on the lacquered laminate surface.

In addition, the Nordic and Sylt programs have new colours in genuine lacquer finish. Nordic is supplemented by the Lacquer, perfect
honed black design. With Sylt, Lacquer, perfect honed sand brings natural elegance to the cottage style. Both front extensions impress
with their exclusive look and their practical suitability for everyday use thanks to our anti-fingerprint coating.
The cottage style segment also gets an upgrade with new design elements such as a breakfast unit, shelf units with wine crosses, glass
cabinets with ribbed glass or wall shelves with console supports.

With the Sakura design line, we are picking up on the popular Japandi style – a sought-after combination of Japanese clarity and
Scandinavian cosiness. The elegant frame design in Como oak combines minimalist lines with modern cosiness. The delicate 16 mm frame
is created thanks to the use of extra-deep upright panels and cover shelves; the Como oak bar handle stylishly rounds off the overall look.

The trend towards a minimalist design opens up exciting possibilities for modern planning. With Line ONE, the handleless design focuses
on a clear horizontal line for kitchens with surfaces that are as calm as possible. Line ONE combines the design versatility of Line N with
the nBOX drawer system’s practical push-to-open technology, enabling a reduction to just one continuous recessed handle line under the
worktop. Handleless plans will also be supplemented by LED lighting for the vertical recessed handle for 2026. This detail creates even
more atmosphere and provides elegant, indirect lighting accents.

Contemporary design, highly suitable in everyday use and new levels of flexibility: With nine new décors, our worktop range offers even
more scope for customised designs. The design variety of Slim Line is also growing: In addition to the popular Como oak décor, three Xtra
honed designs are now also available. A new worktop in an elegant 25 mm thickness opens up additional design options in five décors.
Slim design for greater possibilities. Whether in the new 25 mm thickness or in our 16 mm Slim Line – the slim look now also offers the
full working freedom of our 38 mm standard worktops. Bevelled or rounded edges are therefore available in all three worktop thicknesses.

For 2026, we are also expanding our nBOX to include new, stylish design options: Design covers and profiles are also available in Milano
walnut and Metallic anthracite. The glass sides for front and internal pull-outs are now available in Parsol glass, bronze.
