Quality and function

The high quality design of the carcase elements in addition to the well-
thought out fittings technology is the result of many years of experience and
continual process improvements in the production flow.
All the materials and functioning fittings we use are tested in our in-house
QM laboratory to ensure a minimum operating life of 15 years.
