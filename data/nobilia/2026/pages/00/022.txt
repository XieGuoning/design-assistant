Worktops and upright panel systems
 Worktops
 • Superior quality triple layer chipboard surfaced with durable plastic laminate panels, 38 mm and 25 mm high
 • Heat resistant up to 180°C with dry heat and up to 100°C with moist heat (DIN 68930)
 • Worktop surfaces demonstrate a high degree of resistance to scratching and abrasion in accordance with DIN standard 68930
 • Lateral cut edges are provided with décor edging
 • The bottom face of the worktop is surfaced with a water repellent, resin impregnated paper and has a moisture protection seal along the rear edge
 • All worktops are also supplied with a 9 metre long sealing strip for worktop cut-outs and for insulation of the worktop above dishwashers
 • Please follow the care instructions and always use coasters and chopping boards. Hot pans should not be placed directly on the worktop. Do not cut directly
   on the surface.

 Please note: If items are used for the kitchen work area that are not referred to as worktops in this sales manual,
 these only meet the requirements of DIN 68930 for “other surfaces”.
