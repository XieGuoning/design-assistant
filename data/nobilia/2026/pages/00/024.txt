Quartz worktops and niche cladding designs

 Quartz worktops impress with their high quality, natural-looking appearance
 and exceptional usability.
 Their advantages: Even without special waterproofing, quartz is already
 waterproof.
 The surfaces are particularly easy to clean and hygienic. This makes them
 perfect for kitchen and bathroom designs. Quartz worktops are available in
 the thickness 12 and 38 mm.
 The corresponding niche cladding is 12 mm thick. As with the Xtra worktop,
 flush-mounted installation of technically approved hot plates and sinks is
 possible.

 Everything from one source!
 This basic principle also applies to quartz worktops:
 Worktops and niches are supplied along with the entire consignment within
 the standard delivery time.
 One order. One delivery!
