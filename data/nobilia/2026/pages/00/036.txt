Tall units with a full-length door
Whether in kitchens, living rooms, wardrobes or utility rooms: our tall units with a full-length door are highly versatile and will adapt to your specific needs. They
help you keep your space clean and tidy without compromising on design.

                                                                                              • The full-length, gap-free door offers practical storage space and adds
                                                                                                aesthetic appeal thanks to its large uninterrupted surface.
                                                                                              • Easy access to the entire storage space without opening multiple doors

FurnSpin - a quick-change artist with added value
With the innovative pivot-swivel hinge, the FurnSpin end unit sets new standards in terms of functionality and design.
The inside of the unit can be elegantly turned completely outwards, transforming it into an open shelf unit in no time at all.
Inside becomes outside, and vice versa. This fascinating change in design gives the room a completely new look depending on the side – without any open
doors or flaps in the way. Concealed hinges, soft-close mechanism and push-to-open function combine elegant design with maximum convenience.



 • 600 mm wide base unit, heights: 720 and 792 mm
 • can only be planned with 100 and 150 mm plinth heights
 • Pivot-swivel hinge with 1 fixed shelf in handleless design,
   with PTO and latching mechanism
 • Can be planned at the end of an island or row
 • FurnSpin cannot be planned freely and needs to be screwed to the
   neighbouring units.
 • Designs next to base units are not permitted
 • For island plans, a joinable unit with a minimum carcase depth of 350 mm
   must be planned at the rear.
 • The FurnSpin is available in a clearly defined range of fronts. The
   selected front colour automatically determines the matching carcase
   colour.
