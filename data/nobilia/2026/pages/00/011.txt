The nobilia nBOX system offers customisable design options featuring design profiles and covers in five trend colours: A minimalist design in Slate grey, a
tranquil natural look in Sierra oak, Milano walnut or an exclusive extravagant style in Bronze or Anthracite metallic.
Attaching and replacing covers and profiles is simple and requires no tools. With these options, nBOX has a matching interior design for any lifestyle and any
space.
