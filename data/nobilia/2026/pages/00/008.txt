                                                          More Design. More Comfort. More Choices.
                                                          The nBOX system features a straight-lined, minimalist design in the trend colour
                                                          Slate grey. The narrow drawer frame of only 10.7 millimetres melds seamlessly into the
                                                          overall space. The innovative underframe rail is installed with maximum precision and
                                                          becomes completely invisible for the viewer.
                                                          The pull-outs come standard with a rectangular railing. Dark slate grey exudes
                                                          elegance and is the foundation for an adaptation of the entire interior range. The
                                                          premium design is a standard feature for this series.

                                                          As an option, equipment variants with a glass side or a closed pull-out box are also
                                                          available. Additional customisation options such as design profiles and covers can be
                                                          fastened to the side frames. The nBOX system permits both aesthetic and functional
                                                          upgrades to adapt it to the customer´s requirements.

                                                          nBOX accessory options can be planned and ordered for either individual items or the
                                                          entire commission. If you wish to order them for the entire commission, the surcharges
                                                          can be added to the order header data when placing the order.
                                                          This permits quick and convenient order processing.
