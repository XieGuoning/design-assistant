  The new 20 mm plinth height
  • For the 2026 collection, nobilia has added the 20 mm plinth height that
    can be used in the living room with an installation height from 20 mm and
    up to 28 mm in the kitchen.

    heights for types with a plinth version and the surcharge for sideboard version

    adapters are installed on site.
  • If base units are planned stacked on top of each other at a special height,
