Real ceramic material. Award-winning design.

Xtra Ceramic worktops are defined by their high-quality look and their pleas-
antly cool surface. The worktops are equally popular for kitchens and bath-
rooms thanks to their special aesthetic appearance and pleasant feel. The
surface is hygienic and without pores, which makes it exceptionally easy to
maintain.

The Xtra Ceramic worktop has an innovative sandwich structure. The worktop
with a total thickness of 16 mm consists of 6 mm of solid ceramic material on
a 10 mm lightweight backing layer of recycled PET. This gives
Xtra Ceramic a comparatively low weight, making it easy to install.

Xtra Ceramic will fascinate you with its authentic look and feel and its
impact-resistant design edge. Hot plates and sinks can be flush-mounted.
On top of that, this innovative product is waterproof and hygienic. This
allows it to meet the most stringent demands in kitchens and even in
bathrooms. Xtra Ceramic is available as a worktop and niche.                        INNOVATIVE SANDWICH STRUCTURE
