                                                             More unique options for internal drawers and pull-outs
                                                             The innovative nBOX drawer system gives you more options to customise and design your
                                                             plans in unique ways. The new nBOX lets you adapt internal drawers and pull-outs to create
                                                             your dream design.

                                                             In addition to different versions of the pull-out side equipped with a railing, railing and glass
                                                             side, or a high pull-out box, internal drawers and pull-outs also permit adaptation of the front
                                                             panel for improved appearance and function.
                                                             The internal drawers and pull-outs come standard with a wooden panel. A design glass
                                                             panel and a closed design panel made of plastic are available as alternative options.
