 Breakfast unit BKFST*

 The elegant breakfast unit combines functionality with a cosy design.
 Available in 2 widths, it creates the impressive look shown with plenty
 of storage when placed in a duo.

 the cottage style.
 •     Widths: 500 and 600 mm
 •     Insertable shelves and accent back panel behind the upper door
       in Sierra oak
 •     Delivery includes 1 door shelf set in solid oak wood.
 Available in all tall unit heights and all front ranges.

 The oak door shelf set TRSET50/60E for tall units is also available
 separately as an accessory, see accessories list, Tall unit accessories
