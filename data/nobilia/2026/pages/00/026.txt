 AVENTOS - increased freedom of movement, greater variety

 The AVENTOS flap system brings increased freedom of movement to the
 wall unit. Even large and heavy flaps move easily. AVENTOS is especially
 practical because the freedom of movement and headroom of the kitchen
 user is maintained at all times.
 Independent of the momentum, all AVENTOS flaps always close gently and
 quietly.


 With SERVO-DRIVE, the electric motion support system, swivel flap doors
 and folding/lifting doors can be opened with the slightest touch, and closed
 again by pressing once on the easy-to-reach switch inside the carcase.
 The Servo-Drive equipment is optionally available for a surcharge.
