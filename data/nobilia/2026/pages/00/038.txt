Inviting design elements in detail

  Dining tables
 • 4 table models with frames in powdered steel in the colour 010 Black,
   incl. glide adapters.
 • The table tops are 24 mm thick and 950 mm deep.
 • Available in 5 table top décors.
 • Each table model is available in 2 designs.
   3 fixed dimensions of 1600, 1800 and 2000 mm length or as pull-out
   variant with 500 mm wide attachable or insertable boards.
 • LONDON and PORTO dining tables are available with butterfly hinged
   insert and ETB-POR frontslide pull-out
 • Depending on the table model, the maximum pull-out length ranges
   from 2100 mm to 3000 mm.


 Chairs and bar stools
 • 5 chair series, available as chair or armchair with frames in the colour
   010 Black, with seat height: 480 mm, incl. slide adapter.
 • The series “Cross” also includes a bar stool with a seat height
   of 650 mm and a tall stool with a seat height of 790 mm.
 • The chair series feature different frame designs: free-swinging,
   cross sled frame and central foot with 360° turning function.
 • The seat covers are available in 4 colours.
   For available colours and specifications per model, see chapter 9
