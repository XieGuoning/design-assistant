Pure individuality – designing with glass and back panel concepts

The glass wall and Air tall units combine modern transparency with technical sophistication. The delicate aluminium frame in black impresses thanks to its
integrated handle profile and creates a minimalist, elegant look.
A special highlight: the hinges are completely concealed in the narrow aluminium profile – Air does not only look light and modern, but also has a high-quality
finish. The units can optionally be fitted with an accent back panel. This creates specific highlights and shows off your favourite pieces at their best – especially
in combination with interior lighting Hide (LSIDH*).
• Glass wall and Air tall units with aluminium framed door with integrated
  handle profile in 035 grey glass and glass shelves
• Side depth: 350 mm, total depth: 379 mm
• Wall units WGAIR in the widths of 300, 450, 500 and 600 mm,
  heights: 720, 792, 864 and 1296 mm
• Tall units HGAIR in the widths 300 and 600 mm,
  all tall unit heights in carcase depth 350 mm
• Air glass cabinets are supplied in the carcase exterior decor on the
  inside. Additional interior design options are available with the MP-ARW
  accent back panel at a surcharge.

You will find the Air unit types in chapter 7, Wall units and in chapter 6 in
the respective tall unit heights.




Wall and tall units with Ambia framed glass door
Our Ambia glass units combine proven functionality with an exciting approach to design. Key features are asymmetrical glass cut-outs, which break up the classic
unit image and set stylish accents.
Additional design options such as an accent rear panel in the unit or wall panels which can be freely attached to the wall behind the furniture create a cosy
atmosphere with contemporary style.

• Wall and tall units with Ambia glass doors in various cut-out sizes and positions are
  available in the widths 450, 500 and 600 mm.
• You can find the matching LED unit interior light Hide LSIDHW-43 in the accessories,
  chapter Lighting.
• Optionally with accent rear panel MP-ARW at a surcharge, see chapter Surcharge
  and optional features.
• Can be optionally combined with wall panel for customised planning WPANW* /
  WPANS*
  You can find additional notes and information on the Ambia glass units in chapter 9
  More than kitchen.




MP-ARW accent back panel
Available as an optional extra at a surcharge, the accent back panel covers the back
panel area of glass cabinet units behind the glass cut-out and therefore offers even
more design freedom for impressive contrasts or harmonious colour transitions.
• Available for the type range with Air or Ambia doors and for single-door wall units
  with (bar) flap with framed glass door
• Structure direction vertical


Wall panel for individual room planning WPANW / WPANS
The WPANW / WPANS wall panel's variable dimensions offer maximum freedom of
design and allow it to be integrated perfectly into modern room concepts.
• Available with horizontal grain WPANW or vertical grain WPANS, matches the accent
  back wall MP-ARW
• Optionally available with notch NVAUSK at the bottom left or bottom right at a
  surcharge.
• Optionally also with LED light design MP-WPANL (6495)
You can find further notes and information in chapter 9 More than kitchen.
