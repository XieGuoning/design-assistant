Genuine wood veneer stand-alone furniture in Native Oak

Our genuine wood veneer furniture in Native Oak brushed offers plenty of design options for a fully coordinated living space. These pieces of furniture are a
great addition to any room - be it as a sideboard, glass cabinet, wall unit, tall unit or midi unit. Matching tables made of the same veneer add to the overall
image and give the space a classy atmosphere. Genuine wood veneer furniture is a great eye-catcher, not just as a complete arrangement, but also as stand-
alone furniture that truly gets to shine when combined with the uni-coloured unit elements of our latest collection.
You can find the range of types in our More than kitchen chapter, chapter 9.
