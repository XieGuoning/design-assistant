 Every individual is unique and therefore requires a worktop height adapted to their needs. Our system is designed to permit ergonomic adaptation of working
 heights and individual storage space designs in a grid of 144 mm. Combined with different worktop thicknesses and plinth heights this system makes it possible
 to flexibly adapt each design to the customer’s needs.
