 Sensys – The hinge with perfect cushioning and consummate design.

 Our kitchens are standard equipped with Sensys hinges. Sensys
 represents the newest generation of hinge technology: The cushioning
 is invisibly integrated in the hinge. An innovation, which meets the highest
 standards in comfort and functionality. The doors close with just a
 light movement of your hand – smoothly and noiselessly. The uniquely
 large self-closing device available upwards of approx. 35°, Sensys
 provides an exclusive closing experience.
