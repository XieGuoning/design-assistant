Xtra – the worktop for extra wear and tear

Xtra worktops captivate with their very distinctive 3D surface texture, which
creates an authentic looking natural stone and wood appearance. Matching
upright panels and niche cladding supplement the possibilities for surround
designs in this look.

Thanks to their innovative multiple layered structure, not only do Xtra worktops
look stylish, they also provide practical advantages and enable flush mounting
of sinks and hot plates.

Xtra worktops are available up to a maximum length of 4000 mm. The standard
edge version is décor edge (D-edge).
