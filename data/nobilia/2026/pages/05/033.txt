                                                              Sakura design line
The combination of Japanese simplicity and Scandinavian warmth - that is the essence of the Japandi style. Our kitchen and bathroom furniture reinterprets this
aesthetic and focuses on a design that conveys both calm and structure.
The deep Como oak upright panels are more than just a design detail: They frame the furniture as though it were a picture, giving it a striking visual presence in
the room. The result is an organised, almost architectural expression reminiscent of the precise lines of traditional Japanese interiors.
This concept is complemented by the horizontal screw-on bar handles, which further emphasise the design. The wood-look frame look is the constant element of
our Sakura design line and can be customised with fronts of your choice. The concept can be applied to all smooth, plain-coloured fronts and our wood decors
and opens up a wide range of design options for you - for your personal interpretation of the Japanese-Scandinavian style of living.




              General information        • The protruding 16 mm thick Sakura upright panels in Como oak reproduction achieve a modern, narrow frame look in
                                           combination with the 315 screw-on bar handle in Como oak. Can be designed using all smooth fronts.
                                         • Not available for the following programs: Cascada 684, Frame 419, Inline 526, Nordic 782, Pura 917 and Sylt 706
                                         • Upright panels with 42 mm carcase projection and top shelf with a projection of 44 mm
                                         • UWZ16 / HWZ16 intermediate upright panels can be used from the standard register upright panels for the Sakura
                                           design line. The same also applies to UWR16 spatial design upright and UWAV16 uprights at end of run for advanced
                                           solutions if these are required in different depth dimensions.

              Planning information       • The screw-on bar handle for panels / pull-outs / drawers / flaps is always supplied in the front width.
                                           Fixed panels and pinned fronts are also fitted with a screw-on bar handle.
                                           The screw-on bar handle moulding on hinged doors is indented by 50 mm on the stop side.
                                         • The opening angle limiter must always be set to 85° OWB85 (6328) for hinged doors due to the protruding upright
                                           panel. This also applies to inbuilt refrigerator and freezer units with a tandem door fitting.
                                         • For folding lift-door units, the opening angle limiter must always be designed at 83° OBK-HF (6336) due to the
                                           protruding KBWS16 top shelf cornice.
