                         Planning Information for Corner Solutions



                                         Base units | Corner solutions
                            • All corner units are equipped with an angle filler, which is cut and bolted at the factory. The planning dimensions
                              must be quoted with the order. Adjustments on site are not possible.
                            • Angle changes above or below 90° are not permitted.
                            • Planning dimension changes from minus 20 mm to plus 100 mm are possible at the factory.
                            • A shortened carcase face for affixing to adjacent units is included with the units.
                            • Door hinge side is also the face side.
                            • Specify right or left, to ensure that the corner base unit is delivered with the correct hinge side.
                            • Planning a dishwasher adjacent to a corner unit requires the angle filler with a side depth of 561 mm
