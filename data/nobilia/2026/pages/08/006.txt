Line N Curve end unit
• In the Senso 357 and Easytouch 945 programs, the Curve end units stand for
  a new image and design language. Through their curving frontal look, they
  offer the perfect transition to the joint dimension of tall units with a vertical
  Line N profile.
• The end units open up harmonic planning possibilities for end and
  island planning.
• The end units are available in widths of 450, 600 and 900 mm and in the
  heights: 360, 432, 720 and 792 mm.
• For a surcharge, MP-NGML / MP-NGMLC, the units are available with LED
  lighting in the L and C profiles.
• The stop must absolutely be indicated in the order (stop side = closed front
  end).
• The height and width cannot be adjusted.
• The Curve type range has been extended for Line ONE:
  Here too, the front elements that are equipped with mechanical opening
  support are labelled "PTOSA" in the sketches and are also marked with the
  following symbol.
