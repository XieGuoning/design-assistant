                                     Product Features & Planning Information


The handle profile
• The handle profile is made of anodised aluminium and is available in
  eleven colours: Brushed stainless steel finish, Alpine white, White, Satin grey,
  Sand, Macchiato, Taupe grey, Slate grey, Black, Bronze metallic coloured
  and Yellow gold coloured.
• End caps as well as inner and outer corners are made of plastic in a matching
  colour.
• The maximum profile length for horizontal recessed handles is 4000 mm.
• Vertical recessed handles are supplied in suitable lengths for
  highboards (-XL) and tall units in heights 1, 1 XL, 2, 2 XL, 3 and 3 XL.
• The horizontal handle profile as well as the required mounting materials,
  90° corners and end caps are included in the unit price. Vertical recessed
  handles are individually planned and invoiced (s. page M 8.9).
• Carcase side panels are milled and thus ready for mounting the recessed
  handles.
• Handle profiles are cut to size and ready for mounting, with straight cuts and
  mitre cuts for inner and outer corners.
