Push-to-open PTOSA




                                          Line ONE combines Line N's design versatility with the nBOX drawer system's practical push-to-open technology.
                                          As a variant of the LINE N range, LINE ONE enables a reduction to a continuous, horizontal line management –
                                          without sacrificing the convenience of drawers and pull-outs.
                                          Base unit doors and top pull-outs and drawers are conveniently opened using the recessed handle, while the
                                          bottom pull-outs and drawers are fitted with "push-to-open" PTOSA at the factory and can be opened by lightly
                                          pressing the front. These options are also continued in the highboard and tall unit areas. In addition, doors (e.g.
                                          above the appliance alcoves) are equipped with the "push-to-open" PTO mechanical opening support mechanism
                                          as standard.



                   The product range      • Line ONE types are available in the same full design range as Line N:
                                            All fronts, carcase colours, recessed handles, upright panels, etc. are available for Line ONE.
                                          • Base unit heights: 720 and 792 mm as well as in the highboard and all tall unit heights
                                          • The defined range of types can be combined with items from the familiar LINE N range.
                                            LINE N types with only one recessed handle, such as hinge-door units or types with internal drawers and
                                            pull-outs, can also be designed in combination with LINE ONE types.
                                          • The Curve end fronts are also available for Line ONE.
                                          • The Line ONE types are listed within the respective Line N chapters and follow the familiar structure of the
                                            Line N types.
                                            The first letter of the abbreviations differ:
                                            N = LINE N (e.g. NU2SA)
                                            L = LINE ONE (e.g. LU2SA)
                                          • The front elements of the Line ONE types that are equipped with mechanical opening support are labelled
                                            "PTOSA" / "PTO" in the sketches and are also marked with the following symbol.

                          Block pricing   • The 2026 kitchen block pricing method also applies to Line ONE kitchens, see page M 0.4.
                                          • The Line ONE range of units belongs to the gross furniture pricing group and can be offset with all furniture
                                            pieces from the standard product range.
                                          • Line ONE kitchens can be combined with all accessories and electrical appliances as well as furniture pieces
                                            from the standard product range.
