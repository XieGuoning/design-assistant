                                   Product Features & Planning Information


Planning with dishwashers
• Only fully integrated dishwashers can be planned.
• A minimum plinth height of 150 mm is required for planning dishwashers
  with continuous recessed handle (NGSB*).
• A minimum plinth height of 100 mm is required for planning dishwashers
  with discontinuous recessed handle (NGGSB*).
• Dishwashers with continuous recessed handle (L profile) are installed
  beneath the recessed handle. The useable size of the recess is thus
  reduced by 50 mm. This enables a continuous LED light design for the
  recessed handle (MP-NGML).
• Dishwashers with discontinuous recessed handle (L profile) are installed
  directly beneath the worktop. The recess is totally useable. A continuous
  LED light design for the recessed handle (MP-NGML) is not possible in this
  case, and must be interrupted in the area of the dishwasher.                  NGSB* for appliance heights
• For appliance fronts NGGSB* with discontinuous recessed handles (L or         805 - 820 mm
  C profile), the recessed handles of the adjacent base or tall units will be
  provided with end caps.
• Include an end upright when planning a dishwasher at the end of run.
• A special front upright is available in addition to the end upright when
  planning a continuous recessed handle.

Planning with dishwashers                 in XL height
• Only fully integrated dishwashers can be planned.
• A minimum plinth height of 70 mm is required for planning dishwashers with
  continuous recessed handle (NGSB*- X) and discontinuous recessed handle
  (NGGSB*-X).

  is thus 72 mm smaller. This enables a continuous LED light design for the
  recessed handle (MP-NGML).

  directly beneath the worktop. The recess is totally useable. A continuous
  LED light design for the recessed handle (MP-NGML) is not possible in this
