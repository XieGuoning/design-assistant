Tall units for vertical recessed handles
• Tall units for vertical recessed handles can be used specially for built-in
  fridge-freezer combinations with high appliance apertures, but also for
  larder units. For this, there are highboards and tall units NHS* available in
  heights 1, 1 XL, 2, 2 XL, 3 and 3 XL.
• The carcase side panels on these tall units will be reduced by the profile
  thickness of 17 mm.
• The carcase reduction will be made automatically and, depending on the
  version planned, will be performed on the right, left or both sides.
• Vertical recessed handles are individually planned and invoiced. These are
                                                                                                                 recessed handle between units NGMZ*
  not included in the unit prices.
• The vertical recessed handle is available as intermediate and end profile.
  The planning width is 25 mm.
• A single tall unit with vertical recessed handle can only be planned with end
  uprights. The planning width of the handle profile is 25 mm (including end
  upright).
• For aesthetic reasons, vertical recessed handles can also be planned at
  the end of a tall unit run. An upright panel is necessary when planning at
  end of run.
• An end upright is necessary when planning adjacent base units. A continuous
  visual line is not possible with adjacent base units.
• Combinations between tall units with both horizontal and vertical recessed
  handles are not possible.
• Dishwashers, raised dishwashers, built-under fridge and freezer housing
  units, automatic washer-dryers are not possible with vertical recessed
  handles.

Fastening strips for ovens , microwaves, steam ovens, compact appliances
The fastening strips included in the scope of delivery for installing ovens,
microwaves, steam ovens and compact appliances are available in the colours
014 Stainless steel appearance and 010 Black.
Please indicate the desired colour when placing your order. If no colour is
specified in the order, the fastening strips in 014 Stainless steel appearance
will be supplied.


Ceiling-height designs with continuous recessed handle
Designs with a vertical recessed handle now also permit ceiling-height
designs. Suitable dresser units – such as a height of 360 here – are
placed on the tall units and connected with a continuous recessed handle
(NGMA300 & NGMZ300).
