          Planning Information for Corner Solutions



                         Wall units | Corner solutions
            Planning possibilities for island solutions
            • Corner wall units in wall unit heights 576, 720, 792 and 864 mm can be variably planned with all corner base units.
            • The vertical line formed by the base units should always line up or at the very least be achieved by the next unit.
            Attention with cooker hoods adjacent to the corner wall unit 650 x 650 mm:
            • The first opening door cannot be directly adjacent to the cooker hood, since the depth of the cooker hood would
              prevent it from opening! In this case a design with a 1-door wall corner unit is recommended.

                                    Island Solutions
            Planning possibilities for island solutions
            • Island solutions can be planned in four standard depths:
               1. In combination with base unit depth of 561 mm carcase depths of 202 mm, 350 mm, 461 mm and 561 mm can be
                  planned.
               2. As side end of run we recommend end uprights for room designs in planning depths 807 mm, 955 mm, 1066 mm
                  and 1166 mm.
               3. Additional front end uprights can be planned for all-round recessed handles. The planning dimension of the front
                  uprights is 50 mm. The front uprights are available in four fixed depths (see Ill.).
            • The worktop overhang at the side amounts to 1.5 mm for all versions. On quartz and ceramic worktops, the overhang
              amounts to 2 mm at the side.
            • Free-standing island solutions generally require a safety bracket for free-standing base units (see chapter index).
            • For dishwasher fronts with spacer frames, the carcase sides of the rear units may need to be shortened. Attention, no
              built-under appliances can be planned at the rear!
