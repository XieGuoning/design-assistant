                                      Product Features & Planning Information


Recommended worktop depth and overhang
• Base units and tall units must generally be protruded by 15 mm to accom-
  modate various built-in appliances.
• The worktop overhang for                  kitchens amounts to 3 mm at the
  front and 1.5 mm at the side. On quartz and ceramic worktops, the overhang
  amounts to 2 mm at the side.
• Spacers for mounting are supplied with the units.
• In conjunction with the standard worktop depth of 600 mm, hobs, built-in sinks
  and dishwashers can be planned without complications - even when com-
  bined with niche cladding.
• The visible carcase side can be finished in three different ways:
  with end uprights (598 mm deep), front uprights (597 mm deep) or a cover
  profile with a stainless steel appearance.




Planned end of run
Visible carcase sides as well as the planning distance to the wall can be
faced in three ways:

• End uprights in all décor groups with a depth of 598 mm are available for ends
  (see chapter 5 Upright panels).
• Due to front end uprights NUWF / NHWF with L profile, the recessed handle
  can appear to be the end of run if visually traced back along the carcase.
  Front uprights are available with grain/motif direction on both faces for
  island solutions. Front uprights are available in all              front designs.
• For a more cost effective solution, a stainless steel finish cover profile, NADP*
  (for 15 mm depth compensation) is available.
