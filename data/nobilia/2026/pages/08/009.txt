                                    Product Features & Planning Information


Optional lighting of the horizontal recessed handle
• Horizontal recessed handle on base units, highboards, and tall units can be
  optionally equipped with an LED light design for a surcharge. Depending
  on the profile contour – L or C profile – various surcharges are necessary
  (MP-NGML / MP-NGMC), see base unit / tall unit register, reverse side of
  the register sheet .
• For the surcharge MP-NGML, the L profile on base units and highboards
  will be equipped with LED lighting. This aims to light the course of the profile
  directly under the worktop or the top shelf (see Fig. 1).
• To continue the lighting at the height of the worktop line, the surcharge for
  the C profile MP-NGMC must be included in the combination (see Fig. 2).
• As an additional option, the recessed handle lighting of the C profile
  (MP-NGMC) can also be planned above or below the front pull-out height
  (see Fig. 3). This will illuminate both horizontal profile tracks.
• Dishwasher designs with door fronts in a divided look (NG(G)SBT) cannot
  be planned with adjacent base units with a lighted C-profile. In this case,
  dishwasher designs with a full length look (NG(G)SB) are required.
• Light designs can be selected individually for connected planning units. In
  this way, e.g. a single run out of multiple kitchen runs can be ordered with
  a lighted design.
• With both surcharges, the price per 100 mm or part thereof will be charged.
